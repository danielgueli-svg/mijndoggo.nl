Cockapoo

Cockapoo puppies for Sale in the UK

Cockapoos, also fondly known as Cockerpoos, Cockerdoodles, or Spoodles, are a delightful blend of the Cocker Spaniel and Poodle. This charming crossbreed was first developed in the 1960s with the aim of combining the intelligence of a Poodle with the affectionate nature of a Cocker Spaniel. Highly cherished in the UK, Cockapoos are known for their playful disposition and lively personalities, making them standout companions for families and singles alike. Their coats can be straight, wavy, or curly, requiring regular grooming. With their friendly and sociable nature, Cockapoos often thrive in interactive environments.
